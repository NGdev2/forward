# Forward — Google Play listing (EN)

**App name (30):** Forward: Endless Road RPG

**Short description (80):**
Run an endless road, choose your lane, fight bosses, and gear up your hero.

**Full description (4000):**
The road never ends — and neither does the loot.

FORWARD is a bite-sized RPG runner. Your hero runs down an endless road and every few seconds three lanes open up ahead: a monster, a chest, a campfire, a shrine, a trader, or something unknown. Pick a lane. Live with it.

⚔️ TACTICAL TURN-BASED FIGHTS
Every enemy telegraphs its next move. Strike, unleash weapon skills, brace, drink a potion — and PARRY: time your tap to the moment the blow lands to cut its damage to a third. Read the full fight log to see exactly who did what.

🐺 SIX RELIC SETS, HUNDREDS OF ITEMS
Weapons, shields, helms, armour, boots and trinkets in six rarities, each with random affixes. Collect Wolfpack, Ironbound, Emberforged, Tidecaller, Voidtouched and Dawnward set pieces for powerful bonuses — and matching fire, water, void and light effects in battle.

👑 TWENTY BOSSES, MANY WORLDS
Fill the road meter and the boss blocks the way. Each boss has phases, passives and a signature move. Beat it and the road turns into a new biome: swamps, frozen passes, volcanic wastes, spring meadows, storm coasts and stranger places.

📈 REAL PROGRESSION
Level up, spend talent points, and watch your hero's look change with every piece you equip. Dynamic music follows the danger.

No account needed and no forced ads — optional rewarded ads only, when you choose to watch one. Plays offline.
